The mathematics behind the journey

Coordinate Transformation Formulation

For any starting geodetic position (φ, λ) on a spherical datum:

Antipodal Latitude: φ′ = −φ
Antipodal Longitude: λ′ = wrap(λ + 180°)
Surface Distance: dsurface = π · R ≈ 20,015 km
Core Tunnel Distance: dtunnel = 2 · R ≈ 12,742 km

Solar Time Inversion: Exactly 12 hours difference. When departure experiences solar noon, the antipode is at solar midnight.

Harmonic Gravity Train Model

Under Gauss's law for gravity inside a uniform spherical mass, the restoring gravitational force is directly proportional to radial distance r from the center:

F(r) = −(G · M · m / R3) r = −m ω2 r
ttransit = π √(R / g) ≈ 42 min 12 s (uniform density)
tPREM ≈ 38 min 11 s (realistic core density model)

The Antipode Land Rarity Paradox

While 29.2% of Earth's surface consists of continents and islands, only 4.4% of total land area is antipodal to another landmass.

Continental crust is heavily concentrated in the Northern Hemisphere, while the vast Pacific and Southern Oceans dominate the Southern Hemisphere. Most land points therefore have an antipode in open ocean.
